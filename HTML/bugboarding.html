OVERVIEW

Summary

1v1 your friend on this race down the river. Surf through the waterlilies, or soar over the hills with your leaf glider. Knock your competition off the course by hitting them with your stick. Watch out though, get parried by a leaf shield and you'll be sent flying off instead!

DESIGN CHALLENGES

CHALLENGE 1

Linear Map Design

Players can strafe left and right, but the player and camera never rotate / turn. This makes aiming your attacks simpler. This also means course design is restricted, How will I avoid boring and repetitive course design?


MY APPROACH

Combination of distinct parts

The course is made up of seperate course parts derived from the central theme. This makes the course varied and replayable.

intro part, rocky river
lily pond with bouncy bubbles
meneuvering through the treeroots

CHALLENGE 2

Accessibility with the combat system

I wanted the game to be as accessible as possible for players to make it easy to try out. I also wanted the game to have fun and interesting 3C's to make the game replayable. This is a hard balance to maintain.

MY APPROACH

Small control scheme, utilized to the fullest

All you need to play is either WASD or the arrow keys. I utilized these 4 keys to the fullest. You automatically go forward. Left and Right are used for steering, With up you switch boards

POST MORTEM

What went well?

  • For how much trouble I had with coding the physics and systems, the art in the game is clear enough to sell the picture
  • The surfing is pretty convincing

What would I have done differently?

  • I could've spent more time on the UI and player feedback, this is evident from the playtests I did after release
  • The turning feels really sluggish, some corners in the glass cannon mode (which is activated when you throw your stick away) are near impossible to take
