10 Weeks

BugBoarding

Have you ever watched fallen leaves flow down a river with a friend, and you both bet on one to win the race down the stream? I sure have, The water is gushing down, the leaves are picking up speed! Clashing against the rocks, spinning furiously, the end is near, the stakes could not be lower, and your racer is in the lead! But alas, at the final stretch, your friend sweeps by and takes the win! You look to your right, Your friend is emoting, and you say: "Put me on that leaf, I would’ve closed it out ANY day of the week!" Well, dear river racing enthusiasts, don’t let your dreams be dreams, hop on the leaf, hop on: BugBoarding!
